Section 3Before you start

1Get the repo

git clone https://github.com/yashmstack/patent-extraction
cd patent-extraction

2Run your patent

Follow the repo README. In short:

claude
/run-pipeline <PATENT_ID>          # e.g. WO2024109718A1

When it finishes you have runs/<PATENT_ID>/ with the patent, the markdown Claude reads, and the extracted JSON.

3Make the gold folder

 goldenPatents/<PATENT_ID>/
   input/    <ID>.pdf
             <ID>-enriched-numbered.md     what Claude reads
   output/   reactions.json  compounds.json  pathways.json
   audit/    created by the prompts

ID=<PATENT_ID>
mkdir -p goldenPatents/$ID/{input,output}
cp runs/$ID/input/pdf/*.pdf                             goldenPatents/$ID/input/
cp runs/$ID/input/$ID-enriched-numbered.md              goldenPatents/$ID/input/
cp runs/$ID/output/{reactions,compounds,pathways}.json  goldenPatents/$ID/output/

4Open Claude Code there and run the prompts

cd goldenPatents/<PATENT_ID>
claude

Paste the prompts from prompts/ in number order.

Claude reads the .md, not the PDF: it is translated to English and line-numbered, so every value in the gold points back to a line.

Golden Patents · user manual5 · the prompts

Section 5The prompts, one by one

1

Identify reactions

reactions · flags

Reads the .md alone, never the JSON, and counts every reaction and step, with line numbers.

→ your own list and count
2

Reconcile reactions

reactions · flags

Matches your list against reactions.json by identity, not by name or number.

→ matched · missing · extra → findings.csv
3

Identify compounds

compounds · flags

Reads the .md alone and lists every compound the patent names, with line numbers.

→ your own list and count
4

Reconcile compounds

compounds · flags

Matches your list against compounds.json through identifiers and aliases; flags duplicates.

→ matched · missing · extra → findings.csv
5

Verify reactions

reactions · flags

One record at a time, the whole .md re-read for each, every field checked.

→ reactions_audit.csv
6

Verify compounds

compounds · flags

One record at a time, the whole .md re-read for each, every field checked.

→ compounds_audit.csv
7

Gold check reactions

reactions · edits

Re-derives every field, corrects as it goes, merges duplicates, gives a verdict on every finding.

→ corrected file · yes / no
8

Gold check compounds

compounds · edits

Re-derives every field, corrects as it goes, merges duplicates, gives a verdict on every finding.

→ corrected file · yes / no
9

Adversarial audit

all files · flags

Fresh agents with no context get the finished files and try to prove the gold wrong.

→ findings.csv, with verdicts
10

Identify pathways

pathways · flags

Reads the .md alone and counts the distinct routes, from starting material to product.

→ your own route list and count
11

Reconcile pathways

pathways · flags

Matches your routes against pathways.json, one record per section; flags duplicates.

→ matched · missing · extra → findings.csv
12

Gold check pathways

pathways · edits

Re-projects each step from the gold reactions and compounds, merges routes, checks every field.

→ pathways_audit.csv · yes / no
