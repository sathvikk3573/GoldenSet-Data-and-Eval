Role in the tembotrione story: Intermediate patent — a cleaner 2-step route to the key benzoic-acid intermediate of tembotrione (Examples 1–10) and of tefuryltrione (Examples 11–20) (Jiangxi Tianyu Chemical, 2021).

Inverts the usual order: the methyl ester is cleaved FIRST (base in tertiary alcohol → benzoate salt), and only then is the benzylic bromide etherified. This removes the methoxy group before etherification and so avoids the 2-chloro-3-(methoxymethyl) impurity. Maps to flowchart steps ❷→❹ (shortcut).
